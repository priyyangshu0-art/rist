Overview

The campus placement process recruits students directly from the institute. Final-year diploma/degree students get opportunities with renowned companies through on-campus drives.

Since inception, the T&P Cell has grown from contacts with ~30 companies and 100 placed students to ~100 organizations visiting annually, conducting pre-placement talks, assessments, and interviews.

The Cell provides infrastructure for PPTs, GDs, and interviews, plus training in communication, group discussion, presentation, aptitude, and interview skills. Library resources and the language lab support comprehensive prep.

Vision

To be the bridge between education and industry—training students and enabling placements with reputed organizations.

Mission

Create ample opportunities and achieve excellence in training, placement, and career building for students.

Training

Personality development, CV writing, communication, aptitude, interview techniques, group discussions, and mock GD/PI—mandatory for all students. Eminent speakers deliver sessions on emerging tech; regular practice tests and seminars are organized.

IIPC Cell

The Industry-Institute Partnership Cell reviews placement activities and suggests improvements. It meets at least twice a year—before and after the placement season.
